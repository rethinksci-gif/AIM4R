# Reader

Materials R&D engineers with backgrounds in materials chemistry, scale-up, quality engineering and industrial R&D. Current focus is polymers and recycled plastics; the long-term interest is material intelligence: using data and AI to make R&D decisions faster and more reliably. Do not narrow relevance to one specific plastic or project, and do not force a materials angle onto unrelated AI news.

Readers want to know: what is worth reading, how far the evidence goes, how it relates to their problems, and what to verify next.

# What earns attention

- AI that improves formulation, experiment design, property prediction or R&D decisions, with experimental or out-of-distribution validation.
- Keeping materials data in context: batch, processing history, equipment, test conditions and provenance.
- Robustness across batches, instruments and suppliers; uncertainty estimates; detecting when a model is outside its domain or failing.
- Lab automation, closed-loop experiments, process control, defect detection, and sorting/recycling, especially with real equipment or process data.
- Effects on cost, carbon, yield, cycle time or property stability only when the item reports them.
- Methods from other fields whose transfer can be stated concretely.

# Content-type evidence standards

- Peer-reviewed paper or preprint: material system, dataset size and origin, baselines, validation design (random split vs. held-out batch/supplier/condition, experimental confirmation). A preprint is not peer reviewed; peer review is not industrial validation.
- Industrial case: named organization or line, process, measured before/after metrics, time period, who measured. Vendor claims are allowed but are vendor claims.
- Tool, code or dataset release: what it does, runnable code and docs, license, data provenance, examples on materials or process data.
- Technical analysis: a mechanism, trade-off or constraint explained with sources, not a restatement of headlines.

# Robotics

Robotics enters only when it handles lab operations, inspection, sorting or manufacturing, or carries a clearly stated method transferable to those. Humanoid demos, general manipulation benchmarks and robot business news fail `domain_link`.

# Calibration

- Abstract-only or snippet access limits what can be claimed; score evidence on what was actually supplied.
- A random train/test split on one dataset is weaker evidence than held-out batches, suppliers, instruments or prospective experiments.
- Improved accuracy is not improved cost or yield; do not score industrial value on inferred economics.
- Engagement numbers (stars, likes, upvotes) and brand names are not evidence.
- A high-value research item with little near-term industrial applicability should still score well on the other dimensions; the engine routes it to the exploration track.

Use three to five specific English topic tags.
