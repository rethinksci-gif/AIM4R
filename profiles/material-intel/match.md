# Material intelligence profile

Use this profile for research, engineering and industrial items about AI and data methods in materials and polymer R&D, experiment design and lab automation, manufacturing process control, quality inspection, sorting and recycling, and the data context, validation and uncertainty practices that make such models trustworthy.

Also use it for methods from other fields (statistics, ML, robotics, control) when the item gives a concrete route to materials R&D or manufacturing.

Do not use this profile for general AI model launches, benchmarks or leaderboards, funding and finance, policy news, or software and front-end engineering news.
